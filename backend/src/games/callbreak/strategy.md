# Callbreak — Strategic Guide

You are playing Callbreak, a 4-player, every-player-for-themselves,
13-trick game with a full 52-card deck. Spades are always trump — there is no
concealment and no bidding for trump suit. Each player bids a number of tricks
(1-13, no passing) before play begins; you score for meeting your bid exactly,
lose for falling short, and gain only marginal value for overtricks, so
precision matters more than ambition.

## Core strategic principles

- **Bid on shape, not just high cards.** Count your sure winners: aces, kings
  backed by length, and any spade beyond the first two or three (since spades
  are always trump, even a low spade is a likely trick if you're long in the
  suit and opponents run out).
- **Count spades in play.** There are 13 spades total. If you hold 5+, you
  control trump — bid accordingly and plan to draw out opponents' spades
  early so your long suit runs free later.
- **Track voids.** Once you see a player discard off-suit, they're marked void
  in that suit for the rest of the hand — they'll trump it next time it's led
  unless they're also void in trump.

## Role objectives

- Unlike partnership games, every seat has an individual bid to protect. There
  is no partner to coordinate with, but there is also no one to bail you out —
  play strictly for your own contract.
- If you have already met your bid for the hand, playing further tricks has
  low marginal value (Callbreak awards little for overtricks) — prioritize
  not helping a specific opponent complete their bid instead of grabbing more
  tricks yourself.
- If you are clearly going to fall short of your bid, minimize the shortfall;
  the penalty scales with how far under you land.

## Decision heuristics

- **Leading:** lead your long, strong side-suit before your trumps, so you
  keep control of the hand and can trump in later when a side suit runs out.
- **Following when you cannot follow suit:** if you have already made your
  bid, avoid trumping a trick that would hand another player the exact trick
  they need — let it go if it costs you nothing.
- **Holding trump:** don't spend trump early just because you can; save it to
  cross-ruff or to take control once side suits are exhausted.
- **Endgame counting:** with 3-4 cards left, count exactly who holds which
  remaining card in each suit — Callbreak hands are small enough that perfect
  information is achievable by the last few tricks.
